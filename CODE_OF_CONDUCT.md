# Code of conduct

## A welcoming place to build

Arasaka KDE welcomes people of every experience level, background, identity, and
ability. You deserve to participate without harassment, intimidation, or exclusion.
The cyberpunk aesthetic is for the desktop; kindness is for the people making it.

## What we ask of each other

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking the person behind them.
- Make room for newcomers and different ways of communicating.
- Accept constructive feedback, acknowledge mistakes, and help repair harm.
- Respect people's privacy, time, and boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual attention or imagery directed
at participants, deliberate humiliation, and publishing someone's private
information without permission are not acceptable. Neither is repeated unwanted
contact or retaliation against someone who raises a concern.

## Where this applies

This code applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.
It also applies when someone represents the project publicly. Good-faith technical
disagreement is welcome; personal abuse is not.

## Raise a concern

Contact the maintainer privately at
[cornelius.wichering@gmail.com](mailto:cornelius.wichering@gmail.com), the contact
listed on [@sne11ius's public GitHub profile](https://github.com/sne11ius).
Include what happened and any relevant links. Share only the personal information
needed to understand the report. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

If your concern involves that maintainer, you can use
[GitHub's reporting channels](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
for behavior on GitHub.

## How concerns are handled

Maintainers may clarify expectations, request an apology or correction, remove
harmful content, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. The
response should reflect the seriousness and pattern of behavior. Threats,
harassment, or serious privacy violations can warrant immediate removal.

When practical, the affected participant will receive an explanation and a way
to respond privately. Appeals and requests for clarification can use the contact
above. Retaliation for reporting a concern is itself a violation.

Thank you for helping make this a place where people can learn and build together.
